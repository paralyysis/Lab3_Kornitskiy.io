Cabinet 03 · What is inside

Everything tucked in the drawers

Open a drawer here and the matching one slides out of the cabinet in the room.

  • Every chair, cloud and ear is sculpted from spheres, boxes and capsules when the page loads. There are no model files to download, so the room appears almost instantly.

    • No GLTF or OBJ
    • Rounded boxes
    • Soft shadows

  • Mochi walks on a fixed 60 Hz spring stepper, so the waddle, the squash and the ear flop feel the same on a phone and on a 240 Hz monitor.

    • Squash and stretch
    • Ear springs
    • Collision with furniture

  • Each footstep strikes a synthesized wooden bar tuned to a pentatonic scale, so any walk turns into a small tune. Opening an object rings a glass chime.

    • Web Audio only
    • Pentatonic scale
    • Mute from the top bar

  • A sky fill, one soft key light and the glow from the TV keep Blush, Mint and Lavender readable against a pure white page.

    • Neutral tone mapping
    • Glowing TV
    • Floating shadow
